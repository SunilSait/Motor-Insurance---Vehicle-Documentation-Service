Important Notes

Things to Keep in Mind

Avoid delays and rejections by following these key guidelines before submitting your documents.

Documents Must Be Original

All documents submitted to the RTO must be originals or duly attested copies. Photocopies without attestation are not accepted.

Timely Submission Required

RC transfer must happen within 30 days of purchase. Insurance renewal should be done before the expiry date to avoid survey and penalty fees.

Name & Details Must Match

Owner name, address, and vehicle details on all documents must exactly match the RC book. Discrepancies cause rejection at the RTO.
