Service 02

RC Ownership Transfer

Transferring a vehicle changes hands legally through an RC transfer at the RTO. Our experts handle the entire process — from Form 29/30 preparation to final RC update — so you don't have to visit the RTO even once.

Note: RC transfer must be completed within 30 days of vehicle purchase as per Motor Vehicles Act. Late transfers attract penalty fees.

Transfer Process Includes:

Form 29 & Form 30 Preparation

Transfer notice and application forms prepared accurately as per RTO norms.

Service 04

PUC (Pollution Under Control) Assistance

A valid PUC certificate is mandatory under the Motor Vehicles Act. Traffic police conduct regular checks, and driving without a valid PUC can result in a ₹10,000 fine. We help you get your PUC quickly and without hassle.

PUC certificates contribute to reducing air pollution by ensuring all vehicles on the road meet emission standards.
